## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance and profitability using the Superstore dataset. The objective is to transform transactional data into an interactive executive dashboard that supports business performance monitoring and identifies opportunities for improvement.

**[View Interactive Dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/alejandra.sarmiento7515/viz/SuperstoreSalesProfitAnalytics-PracticalActivity/00ExecutiveDashboard)**

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Compare revenue and profitability across product categories.
- Identify categories with opportunities for profitability improvement.
- Enable interactive analysis through date and region filters.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau Public:** Data visualization and dashboard development.
- **Calculated Fields:** Profit margin calculation.
- **Interactive Filters:** Date and regional performance analysis.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and portfolio management.

## Key Business Insights

**1. Technology leads in revenue and profit**

Technology generates approximately $840K in sales and $146.5K in profit, achieving a 17.4% profit margin.

**2. Office Supplies demonstrates strong profitability**

Office Supplies generates approximately $732K in sales and $126.0K in profit, achieving a 17.2% profit margin.

**3. Furniture presents an opportunity for improvement**

Despite generating approximately $755K in sales, Furniture contributes only $19.7K in profit, with a 2.6% profit margin. This suggests an opportunity to investigate pricing, discounts, product mix, and cost efficiency.
